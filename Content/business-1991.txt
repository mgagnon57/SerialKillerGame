#  WHAT TRADED HERE IN 1991 - THE OWNER'S OWN RULING
#
#  Authored, not derived. Nothing in this project records what was open in
#  Rossville in 1991: the tax roll starts in 2007 and the imagery is 2016. The
#  downtown used to carry the 1913 Sanborn sheet's trades - a jeweller, a meat
#  market, a G.A.R. hall - which is seventy-eight years out and is why they were
#  cleared to numbered handles for this file to fill.
#
#  Keyed by the unit handle in city.txt, NOT by the business name: the build
#  replaces the name with whatever is ruled here, so keying on the display name
#  would break the ruling the moment it took effect.
#
#    unit "Rossville unit 12" kind tavern
#    unit "Rossville unit 12" business "Shorty's"
#    unit "Rossville unit 12" trade "beer and a grill"
#
#  `kind` must be a word kinds.txt answers to - it drives the interior, the jobs,
#  the hours and the animations. `business` and `trade` are prose and are shown.
#
#  Written by the game itself (the place panel). Hand-editing is fine; the next
#  save from the game rewrites the whole file.
# ============================================================================
