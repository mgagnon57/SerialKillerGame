#  WHAT IS NOT A ROAD AND NOT A LOT
#
#  The railway, the water and the schools, from OpenStreetMap, converted to
#  metres about the Chicago x Attica crossing like everything else and clipped
#  to the map.
#
#  Named things in here, so they can be recognised on the ground:
#    the CSX Woodland Subdivision - the line down the east side of the village
#    the North Fork Vermilion River - outside town to the west
#    Middle Branch - the stream out east
#    the ponds behind the school campus, west of the elementary
#    seventeen field DITCHES, which is what eastern Illinois has instead of
#      streams: the land is till plain and drains by being dug.
#
#  RR CROSSINGS, four of them, from OpenStreetMap's own railway=level_crossing nodes - not
#  guessed and not "every street gets one". Henderson, Green, Benton and Attica each cross the
#  CSX line at grade in reality; Holmes, Maple, Gilbert, Stewart, McKibben, Dale, Greenwood,
#  Thompson and Earlcourt do not, whatever the rest of their run looks like on this plan. When
#  a train is simulated, these four are where a car can be caught waiting and nowhere else is.
#
#  `rail` and the waterways are open polylines; `water`, `school` and `crossing` close.
# ============================================================================
