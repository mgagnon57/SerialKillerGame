#  every street was long, every errand was a hike, and the town read as

#    tower and one small apartment terrace. Nine blocks, walkable corner to
#    corner in four minutes, which is what makes it somewhere to go.
#
#    THE HOMES GROW OUT OF IT. Forty cells of houses wrapped round downtown,
#    nearest first. Nobody lives downtown except the one terrace, and that
#    is deliberate: a town where the shops are somewhere else is a town with
#    a morning worth watching.

#    fields, copses, and the places the story happens in.
#
