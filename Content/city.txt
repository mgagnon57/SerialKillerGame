# ============================================================================
#  NORTHGATE
#
#  160 x 120 tiles, one tile = one metre. SMALL AND DENSE ON PURPOSE. The first
#  cut was 240x180 and mostly empty, so the city read as a few blocks marooned in
#  a field. A demo wants everything inside one look.
#
#  THE MODULES DECIDE THE GRID, not taste:
#    - the road tile is 10m, so every road fills exactly one 10m cell row or
#      column and the whole plan snaps to 10.
#    - the townhouse module is 6.1m wide, so a terrace lot is 6 and a terrace is
#      that repeated.
#    - every landmark lot is the pack's own footprint rounded up. School_City is
#      46.2 x 17.9; Hospital_City is 39.2 x 25.8 and gets 40x27.
#
#  THE GROUND IS PAVED, and not only because a city is paved: trees, hedges and
#  bushes are scattered by Prop.ScatterOn, which fires on grass, wood, field and
#  churchyard and on NOTHING else. Paving is what stops a hedge growing down the
#  middle of the avenue, and the parks are where greenery is allowed back.
#
#  Painted in order: terrain, then roads over terrain, then buildings over roads.
# ============================================================================

village Northgate
