# ============================================================================
#  NORTHGATE
#
#  240 x 240 tiles, one tile = one metre. Nine blocks of sixty metres, separated
#  by four roads, crossing at four signalised junctions.
#
#  THE ROAD KIT DECIDES THE GRID, not taste. Prefabs/City/Roads City is built at
#  thirty metres, so a through-road is a THIRTY-METRE CORRIDOR and the plan snaps
#  to thirty. What differs between an arterial and a main road is not the
#  corridor - both are thirty - but how much of it is asphalt:
#
#    freeway    30m corridor, 24m asphalt, two lanes each way, orange centre
#    mainroad   30m corridor, 12m asphalt, one lane each way, bus lay-bys
#    street     10m corridor,  6m asphalt, unmarked
#
#  Because width alone cannot tell a freeway from a mainroad, the class is said
#  outright on an indented `class` line rather than inferred.
#
#  The previous city was 160x120 with 10m roads, which was 6m of asphalt: two
#  lanes and nothing over, no room to pass, turn or stop, and no junction big
#  enough to hold a signal. Everything below is that plan at the scale the road
#  kit was actually drawn for.
#
#  THE MODULES DECIDE THE LOTS:
#    - the townhouse module is 6.1m wide, so a terrace lot is 6 and a terrace is
#      that repeated. Sixty metres of block edge is nine of them and a corner.
#    - every landmark lot is the pack's own measured footprint rounded up.
#      Skyscraper_A is 46.9 x 38.2 and gets 48x39; Casino is 34.5 x 45.4 and
#      gets 35x46. Measure first: a lot smaller than its model is a building
#      standing in the road.
#
#  THE GROUND IS PAVED, and not only because a city is paved: trees, hedges and
#  bushes are scattered by Prop.ScatterOn, which fires on grass, wood, field and
#  churchyard and on NOTHING else. Paving is what stops a hedge growing down the
#  middle of the avenue, and the parks are where greenery is allowed back.
#
#  Painted in order: terrain, then roads over terrain, then places over roads.
#  An open place STAMPS ITS GROUND, so a place laid over a road must have road
#  for its ground or it quietly erases the street - which is what the Elevated
#  did to Second Street in the last plan.
#
#      0    60   90  150 180  240
#  0   +----+====+====+====+====+
#      | WN |  # | CN |  # | EN |
#  60  +----+====+====+====+====+
#      |==== N O R T H G A T E ====|   freeway, four lanes
#  90  +----+====+====+====+====+
#      | WM |  # | CM |  # | EM |
#  150 +----+====+====+====+====+
#      |====  F R A N K L I N  ====|   mainroad, two lanes
#  180 +----+====+====+====+====+
#      | WS |  # | CS |  # | ES |
#  240 +----+====+====+====+====+
#           First     Second
#          mainroad   freeway (and the Elevated over it)
# ============================================================================

village Northgate
size 240 240

# there is no dead end for traffic to have to turn round in.
