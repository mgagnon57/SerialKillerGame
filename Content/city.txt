# ==========================================================================
#  NORTHGATE, laid out as ROSSVILLE, ILLINOIS
#
#  Researched rather than invented. Rossville sits at 40.38N 87.67W in Ross
#  Township, Vermilion County, a hundred miles south of Chicago on Illinois
#  Route 1 - and the things that make it that town rather than any town are:
#
#    CHICAGO STREET IS ROUTE 1. The Dixie Highway runs north-south straight
#    through the middle and every address in the village is numbered north or
#    south of the cross street. It is the reason the place is here at all.
#
#    ATTICA STREET CROSSES IT, and that junction is the ONE set of lights.
#    Everything else is a stop sign, which is now a rule in CitySignals rather
#    than an accident of which corners happen to be paved.
#
#    THE STOREFRONTS ARE THE 100 AND 200 BLOCKS of North and South Chicago -
#    four blocks, both sides, and that is the whole business district.
#
#    THE WATER TOWER IS THE SKYLINE. Not a skyscraper. It is the thing you see
#    from the section road two miles out, and the grain elevator is the other.
#
#  TRUE SCALE, NOT SHRUNK. Rossville's blocks are about 300 feet - 91m - and
#  this project has been on a 90m pitch all along, so the grid below is the
#  real one. Eight blocks by ten, with half a mile of country round it.
#
#  THE COUNTRY IS NEARLY EMPTY OF PEOPLE, and that is correct. Ross Township
#  holds 1,617 against the village's 1,331, so 286 people are spread over 42
#  square miles - about seven to the square mile. Half a mile of country is
#  therefore a handful of farmsteads, not a suburb. It only starts to add up
#  when the map covers the county.
#
#  GROWS NORTH AND SOUTH. Hoopeston is six miles up Route 1 and Alvin five
#  miles down it, so Chicago Street runs the full height of the map and the
#  frame is not centred on anything that would have to move to extend it.
#
#  Generated by tools/relay-rossville.py. MapAudit checks the result either way.
# ==========================================================================
