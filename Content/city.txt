# ==========================================================================
#  ROSSVILLE, ILLINOIS - THE TOWN ON ITS OWN STREET GRID
#
#  Not a grid that looks like Rossville - the actual one, taken from
#  OpenStreetMap and converted to metres about the crossing of Chicago Street
#  and Attica Street. That crossing is where the village numbers its addresses
#  from: East Attica ends and West Attica begins on exactly that longitude,
#  which is how the origin was found rather than assumed.
#
#  TWO THINGS THE REAL DATA GAVE THAT GUESSING WOULD NOT:
#
#    THE TOWN IS MOSTLY EAST OF ROUTE 1 - 780m east of Chicago Street and only
#    224m west. A symmetrical grid would have been wrong in a way you would feel
#    without being able to name it.
#
#    THE SPACING IS IRREGULAR - 104m Chicago to Harrison, 109 to Church, then
#    186 to Summit and 181 to Grove. Blocks average about 113m, not the 91m a
#    textbook 300-foot block gives, and the variation IS the character.
#
#  Several streets change name as they cross Route 1: McKibben east is McKibbin
#  west, Maple is Park Place, Gilbert is Perry, Stewart is Stufflebeam. Each is
#  emitted once under its eastern name, because the town is mostly east.
#
#  ONE SET OF LIGHTS, at Chicago and Attica. CitySignals requires both arms to be
#  a main road, so every other junction in the village is a stop sign.
#
#  THE WATER TOWER AND THE GRAIN ELEVATOR are the skyline. No tower block.
#
#  THE COUNTRY IS NEARLY EMPTY OF PEOPLE and that is right: Ross Township holds
#  1,617 against the village's 1,331, so 286 are spread over 42 square miles.
#  Seven to the square mile. It only adds up at county scale.
#
#  GROWS NORTH AND SOUTH: Chicago Street runs the full height of the map because
#  Hoopeston is six miles up it and Alvin five miles down it.
#
#  BOOTSTRAPPED by tools/relay-rossville.py on 2026-08-02; street positions in
#  tools/rossville-streets.json. THAT TOOL NO LONGER OWNS THIS FILE. Everything
#  since is authored here and here only - the roads moved onto their real rights
#  of way, fifteen alleys, Chicago Street's curve, the school, the downtown, the
#  1991 trades - and re-running the tool would replace the lot with a bootstrap.
#  It refuses to, and needs --write to be argued with. Edit THIS file.
# ==========================================================================

# ---- Illinois Route 1 and the cross street --------------------------------
# `mainroad` is a 30m corridor, one lane each way with lay-bys, which is what a
# two-lane state highway through a village is. Both run the full extent of the map:
# a state route does not stop at the village limit.
# ROUTE 1 AND CHICAGO STREET ARE ONE ROAD, AND IT IS NOT STRAIGHT.
# Outside the village it is Illinois Route 1; inside it, Chicago Street. Same road.
#
# IT RUNS DIAGONALLY THROUGH A SQUARE GRID, and that is not a contradiction - it is
# the whole history of the place. This road is the HUBBARD TRAIL: a footpath from
# 1829, Chicago to Danville, which became Illinois's first state highway in 1833.
# A footpath does not follow section lines. The town was platted square in 1857
# around a trail that was already there, so the grid is cardinal and the road that
# made the town cuts across it at about twenty degrees. See ROSSVILLE-HISTORY.md 1.
#
# Alignment from OpenStreetMap way 22037977 (ref=IL 1), projected into village metres
# about the Chicago/Attica crossing. Measured bearing: a constant +20 degrees from
# north of town down through the crossing and the downtown, then bending south of the
# commercial block - +21 at y1555, +17 at y1645, +13 at y1682, +9 at y1721, +6 from
# y1734 on. That bend south of town centre is the one the owner describes.
#
# THIS WAS BRIEFLY STRAIGHTENED IN ERROR on 2026-08-03 and put back the same day. The
# argument for straightening was that the way carries `tiger:reviewed = no` and
# `tiger:county = Iroquois, IL` while Rossville is in Vermilion, and that every other
# street in town is square to two or three degrees. All of that is true and none of it
# matters: the owner has driven this road his whole life and it curves, and the
# Hubbard Trail explains exactly why a square town has a diagonal highway through it.
# The buildings were what needed moving, not the road.

# ---- the section roads -----------------------------------------------------
# NO SIMULATED RAILROAD AVENUE. The real CSX line is diagonal - it crosses barely
# 130m east of Chicago Street up at York and opens past 1,100m by Earlcourt - and
# the engine's roads are axis-aligned only, so any single vertical line drawn for
# it is wrong for most of the town's latitude by construction. It used to be a
# flat +900m guess, and a quarter of the village's houses turned out to be on the
# wrong side of the real track for their row because of it. The real alignment is
# drawn instead as a `rail` feature in features.txt, pulled from OpenStreetMap,
# which CAN follow the real diagonal because it is decoration and not a road a
# person or a car is simulated walking on. RAILROAD the constant survives, for the
# elevator's siting below, but nothing paves a road along it any more.
#
# The country is surveyed on a mile grid and the gravel section roads follow the
# section lines. Each crosses a main road, so none is an island - an isolated road
# has lanes and no junction at either end, and its traffic drives off the tarmac
# and out of the world.
# THESE FOUR NAMES ARE PLACEHOLDERS AND SHOULD LOOK LIKE IT.
# Vermilion County numbers its rural roads on the Illinois grid - East 3550
# North Road, 3330 North Road, 1050 East Road - where the number is distance
# from a baseline in hundreds of feet. In the fields a road is a number; in town
# it is a surname.
#
# We have exactly ONE anchor: OSM puts `East 3550 North Road` at y 1308-1326,
# eighteen metres off Attica Street, running west off the map. That is not a
# separate road - it is ATTICA'S RURAL CONTINUATION, the same relationship
# Chicago Street has with Route 1, on the other axis.
#
# One anchor is not a grid. The blotter also names 3330 North Road, but that
# would sit some 6.7 km from 3550 on a straight hundreds-of-feet reading, well
# off a 2.4 km map - so either the numbering is not uniform here or those roads
# are outside our bounds, and nothing available settles which. The owner does
# not know the local numbers either.
#
# So they stay `section0` and `crossroad0`. A convincing invented number is
# worse than an obvious placeholder: it would read as researched and be fiction,
# which is exactly how the TIGER curve cost two days.

# ==========================================================================
#  THE HOUSES
#
#  Rossville held 1,331 people in 2010. Units are set by BLOCK AREA rather than
#  flat, because the real grid's blocks are not the same size - Chicago to Harrison
#  is 104m and Church to Summit is 186 - and giving a narrow block the same
#  population as a wide one is exactly how a real grid gets flattened into a fake
#  one. About one household to 1,750 square metres, which lands on the village's
#  own figure without being told it.
#
#  408 HOLMES AVE is on the block between Summit and Grove, north side of Holmes
#  Avenue, 126m north of Attica. Holmes runs east of Chicago Street only, which is
#  why the 400 block is the fourth one east. The house is not yet a place of its
#  own - that is the next piece of work.
# ==========================================================================

# ==========================================================================
#  THE HOUSES, ONE ADDRESS AT A TIME
#
#  Every house is its own place and its NAME IS ITS ADDRESS. That needed no new
#  directive and no new field: `house` is already an alias of the `dwelling` kind
#  (kinds.txt), which is already `home yes`, and WorldBuilder already refuses two
#  places with the same name - so an address is unique by construction and Place
#  already hashes it into a stable key. The town addressed itself.
#
#  THE NUMBERING IS THE REAL CONVENTION. The hundred counts CROSS STREETS out
#  from Route 1, not metres - the 400 block of E Holmes is the fourth one east
#  whether that is 400m or 700, and Rossville's spacing is irregular enough that
#  the difference is not academic. Within the block the lot number counts outward
#  from the crossroads, even on the south side of a street and odd on the north,
#  which is how you can stand on one kerb and know the numbers opposite.
#
#  SO 408 HOLMES AVE IS A DOOR. Fourth lot of the fourth block east, south side
#  of Holmes Avenue. You can walk to it.
#
#  Vacant lots are simply not emitted. A declining village has gaps in its
#  frontage - Rossville has lost a hundred people since 2010 - and a number with
#  no house on it is what that looks like from the street.
# ==========================================================================
