# ============================================================================
#  THE ROADS OF ROSSVILLE, FROM SURVEY RATHER THAN FROM A RULER
#
#  DERIVED AND RE-RUNNABLE - build with tools/build-roads.py. This file does NOT
#  replace the road lines in city.txt; nothing reads it yet. It is the proposal, and
#  the numbers that justify it are below.
#
#  WHAT WAS WRONG. 32 of city.txt's 37 road lines are two points - a straight line
#  ruled from one side of the town to the other. Attica Street is `0,1353 2099,1353`.
#  The 2026-08-04 refit slid each of them bodily onto the nearest parcel-free strip,
#  which is the most a rigid shift can do and no help at all to a road whose SHAPE is
#  wrong - Harrison turns 15.4 degrees at Benton and a straight line cannot.
#
#  SOURCES, one per class of road:
#
#    STREETS - Vermilion County's own centreline layer, 146 segments over Rossville,
#    on the same ArcGIS server that gave us the parcels and the property records. Real
#    surveyed geometry, with address ranges and ZIP 60963 attached. Segments are
#    chained into continuous runs here, since the layer cuts them at every junction.
#
#    ALLEYS - derived from the parcel gaps by tools/derive-alleys.py, because no county
#    maps its alleys. A right of way is the ground nobody owns, so the alley is already
#    in the survey as an absence and only needs tracing out of it.
#
#    CHICAGO STREET - copied from city.txt untouched. It is authored, the owner
#    corrected it personally, and SOURCES-OF-TRUTH.md says it is never regenerated.
#    The measurement backs the ruling: the authored curve runs on private land 0.0% of
#    the time and the county's own Route 1 line manages 3.4%, so replacing it would be
#    a downgrade. Route 1 is dropped as redundant - it is the same road.
#
#  HOW GOOD IS IT. Sampled every 4 m through the platted town, share of centreline
#  running across somebody's lot - which for a public right of way should be zero:
#
#    city.txt as it stands     9.0%
#    this file                 1.6%
#
#  NAMES. The county spells McKibben as `McKidden`; this project's spelling is kept.
#  The county also carries the west-of-Route-1 names the research already predicted -
#  Park, Perry, Stufflebeam, Smith, Watson - as real addressed streets, so they are
#  here as roads in their own right rather than as continuations.
#
#  TWO WIDTHS, DO NOT CONFUSE THEM. The number on a road line is the CORRIDOR the road
#  kit is built to and VillageParser pins to the class - alley 4, street 10, mainroad
#  30 - and declaring any other value is a parse error. The measured RIGHT OF WAY is a
#  different quantity, written in the trailing comment: 20.0 m for a street, 6.0 m for
#  an alley, taken off the parcel gaps. The right of way is what the town has; the
#  corridor is what the tiles draw.
#
#  THREE WIDTHS, NESTED, AND ONLY THE MIDDLE ONE IS THE ROAD:
#    easement  the public land - the gap the lots leave. Measured off the parcels.
#              A Rossville street has 66 ft of it, the same as its main roads.
#    corridor  what the road kit paves: asphalt plus its pavements. Pinned to the class
#              at 33 ft for a street and 13 ft for an alley. Written on the road line.
#    asphalt   measured off the tile mesh at build time by CityStreets, inside that.
#  So a street leaves roughly 16 ft each side of public ground that is not road - verge,
#  utilities, and a walk WHERE THERE IS ONE. Which roads have a sidewalk is not recorded
#  here and is not known per road: the downtown photographs show a concrete walk set back
#  behind a grass verge on Chicago Street, and nothing establishes it street by street.
#  The easement is what a later pass needs to lay one without crossing a lot line.
#
#  FORMAT  road <name> <corridor width, metres> x,y x,y ...   then an indented class,
#          a `carries` where the road's job outranks the ground it has, and, where it
#          could be measured, an easement in metres.
#  Village metres, the same frame as parcels.txt and city.txt.
# ============================================================================
